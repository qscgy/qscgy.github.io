I am currently a postdoc in the [AAI-CTR](https://advanced.aorticimaging.radiology.wisc.edu/) group led by Dr. Nicholas Burris at the University of Wisconsin. I am studying applications of computational geometry to better diagnose and treat diseases of the aorta.

My doctoral dissertation, supervised by Prof. Stephen Pizer at UNC-Chapel Hill, focused on the use of geometric computer vision, machine learning, and 3D reconstruction to improve the accuracy of colonoscopy. Previously, I worked with Prof. Mahdi Bayat of Case Western Reserve University studying non-contrast ultrasound signal processing. Before that, I worked with Prof. Corbin Covault, also of CWRU, to investigate the sources of ultra-high-energy cosmic rays (on the order of 1 EeV energy, which is similar to the kinetic energy of a thrown baseball but in a single subatomic particle).
